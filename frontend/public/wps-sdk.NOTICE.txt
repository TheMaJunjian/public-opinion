WPS WebOffice SDK

wps-sdk.es.js is the unmodified web-office-sdk-v1.1.11.es.js distributed
in the WPS Open Platform multi-dimensional table sample (embed-db-demo).
The sample names the file 1.1.11; the module itself reports V1.1.15.
The sample's package.json identifies its license as ISC.
